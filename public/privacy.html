ExpenseFlow Legal · Stage 1 Offline-First Core

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 · Package ID: com.expenseflow.app

ExpenseFlow ("the app") is a personal expense tracking and budgeting application built around offline-first local storage. This Privacy Policy explains what data ExpenseFlow handles on your device and via optional Firebase services, in alignment with Google Play Developer Policies.

Stage 1 Release Disclosure: In this offline-first core release, your expenses, budgets, purchase plans, challenges, and regional preferences are stored locally on your device. No cloud database stores your financial ledger, and optional Stage 2 cloud add-ons (AI Spending Coach, Cloud Sync, and in-app Play subscriptions) are disabled.

1. Local On-Device Financial Data

Stored Locally Only: Expense transactions, target monthly budgets, purchase affordability plans, savings challenges, currency selection, and country preferences remain in local application storage on your device. Core tracking works completely offline without creating an account, and ExpenseFlow never sells user financial data.

2. Optional ExpenseFlow Account (Firebase Authentication)

Creating an ExpenseFlow account is optional. If you register or sign in inside the app, Google Firebase Authentication (Firebase project expenseflow-ad899) processes your email address, password credentials, and Firebase user ID (UID) to authenticate your session. Passwords are handled directly by Google Firebase Authentication and are never saved in local app storage.

3. Planned Stage 2 Features (Disabled in Stage 1)

ExpenseFlow contains disabled UI placeholders for upcoming Stage 2 features (AI Spending Coach, multi-device cloud sync, and Google Play Billing subscriptions). In Stage 1 builds:

  • No live AI Spending Coach prompts are sent and no Gemini cloud coaching calls are enabled.
  • No Google Play subscription purchases are initiated or processed inside this build.
  • No personal transactions or notes are synchronized to a remote Firestore database.

4. Device Permissions & Optional Usage Analytics

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO / MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS): Used strictly when you tap Voice Expense Entry to transcribe spoken entries using platform/WebView speech recognition. Audio recordings are not stored on disk or uploaded to ExpenseFlow servers.
  • Internet (INTERNET): Used only if you voluntarily sign in/register with Firebase Authentication, delete your Firebase account, or leave optional anonymous usage analytics enabled.
  • Optional Anonymous Usage Analytics (Firebase Analytics): When enabled, Firebase Analytics logs strictly allowlisted, non-financial navigation and action events (e.g., screen views or adding/deleting an entry—never amounts, categories, merchants, or notes). You may opt out anytime inside the app at More > Data Storage & Privacy > Anonymous Usage Analytics.

5. Data Retention, Export & Account Deletion

You retain complete control over both local device records and any optional Firebase Authentication account:

  • In-App Account Deletion: Sign in inside ExpenseFlow, open More > ExpenseFlow Account > Manage / Delete > Delete ExpenseFlow Account, type DELETE (and re-enter your current password if prompted for recent security verification), and confirm. You may optionally check the box to also erase local expenses on your device; otherwise your offline expenses remain intact on your device.
  • External Web Account Deletion (No App Install Required): You can permanently delete your Firebase Authentication account directly from any web browser using our standalone deletion page: Delete ExpenseFlow Account (Web).
  • Local Data Removal Without an Account: If you never created an account, simply export your CSV backup if desired under More > Export Transactions and clear app storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app.
  • What Is Deleted: Account deletion immediately deletes your Firebase Authentication record (email address and UID) in project expenseflow-ad899. Because Stage 1 stores no cloud financial ledger or cloud database records, no server-side expense history exists to retain.

6. Children's Privacy

ExpenseFlow is a personal finance utility intended for adults aged 18 and above and is not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent).

7. Developer Contact

For privacy questions or support regarding ExpenseFlow (com.expenseflow.app), please use the official developer contact email displayed on ExpenseFlow's Google Play Store listing.
