Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 · Effective Date: November 2026

ExpenseFlow ("we", "our", or "the app") provides a mobile-first personal expense tracking application. This Privacy Policy discloses our data collection, processing, and storage practices in accordance with Google Play Developer Policies and global privacy standards.

1. Data Collection & Local Storage

Local On-Device Financial Records: By default, all expense records, budget settings, purchase plans, category tags, and currency preferences entered by the user are stored strictly on the user's local device using client-side persistent storage. We do not automatically upload, store, or sell your individual transaction records to any central server or third party.

2. AI Spending Coach & Data Transmission

ExpenseFlow offers an optional AI Personal Spending Coach powered by Google's Gemini models via a secure backend proxy:

  • Data Minimization: When interacting with the AI Spending Coach, your raw transaction history, transaction IDs, merchant names, individual dates, and personal notes are NEVER transmitted.
  • Aggregated Summary Only: The app transmits only anonymized, high-level summary metrics (current month total spent, target monthly budget, percentage utilization, remaining daily allowance, and top category breakdown percentages) to formulate contextual advice.
  • Server Processing: Data is processed ephemerally on our secure backend solely to fulfill your prompt and is not retained or used to train third-party public models.

3. Device Permissions

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO / MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS): Used exclusively when you tap the Voice Expense Entry button to dictate an expense locally. Audio is transcribed using on-device or native speech recognition APIs and is never stored, recorded, or transmitted to remote audio databases.
  • Internet (INTERNET): Required for Google Play In-App Billing receipt validation, fetching real-time subscription prices, and querying the server-side AI Spending Coach proxy.

4. Subscriptions & Google Play In-App Billing

Subscription purchases (ExpenseFlow Premium Monthly at $14.99/mo and Premium Annual at $109.99/yr) are processed securely through Google Play In-App Billing. We do not collect, view, or store your credit card, debit card, or bank account numbers. Purchase tokens are verified server-side via the official Google Play Developer API strictly to validate active subscription entitlement status.

5. Data Retention & User Deletion Rights

Because your transactions are stored locally, you maintain total ownership and control over your data. You may delete individual expenses at any time or clear all records by resetting app data in your Android device settings. You may also export your complete ledger at any time in universal CSV format.

6. Children's Privacy

ExpenseFlow is designed for general audiences and personal financial budgeting. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

7. Contact Information
