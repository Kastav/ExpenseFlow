ExpenseFlow Account Deletion

Delete Your ExpenseFlow Account

Package: com.expenseflow.app · Firebase Auth Project: expenseflow-ad899

Use this page to permanently delete your ExpenseFlow Firebase Authentication account from any browser, without needing to install or open the Android app.

What happens when you delete your account:
  • Immediately Deleted: Your Firebase Authentication user record (email address, UID, and login session) in project expenseflow-ad899 is permanently removed via Google Identity Toolkit.
  • Local On-Device Records: Expenses, budgets, plans, and preferences stored offline inside your Android device stay on your device until you clear app storage or uninstall ExpenseFlow. (Stage 1 does not store your expense ledger in any cloud database.)
  • In-App Alternative: Inside the ExpenseFlow app, open More > ExpenseFlow Account > Manage / Delete > Delete ExpenseFlow Account.
